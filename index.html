          How detection works

          1. AS7341 samples water-leaving light at 445–680 nm + NIR during the 10-min nadir window.
          2. ESP32-S3 computes NDCI = (R708 − R665) / (R708 + R665) onboard.
          3. Pixels above threshold are tagged; a compact alert packet goes down over LoRa at the next UAE pass.
          4. Full OV5640 images are stored and downlinked selectively for confirmation.

          Telemetry data contract

          v1 · JSON lines · 1 Hz · 115200 baud

          Every packet is one JSON object per line. The same format comes from the simulator, the Tinkercad sketch and the ESP32-S3 flight firmware, so the dashboard never needs to know which one it is talking to. Fields marked required must be in every packet; optional fields show No data until they arrive.

          Ground rules
          
        
            
          
          • A field is stale 3 s after its last update; its tile turns grey and its chart line breaks.
            • 
          
          • No packet for 3 s → link STALE; for 10 s → link LOST (critical alarm).
            • 
          
          • Values outside the sensor's physical range, or of the wrong type, are shown as INVALID and never plotted.
            • 
          
          • Lines that are not valid JSON are counted as rejected and logged.
            • 
          
          • Alarms latch with a UTC timestamp, stay in the history when they clear, and can be acknowledged.

            Arduino stand-in of the flight computer: TMP36 (A0), sun sensor (A1), battery-voltage pot (A3), heater/imaging/downlink LEDs (D13/D7/D4) and antenna-deployment servo (D9). Open the circuit in Tinkercad →
            
        
            Prototype link
            
        
            Flash firmware/marsad_telemetry/marsad_telemetry.ino to the ESP32-S3 prototype, plug it in over USB and press Connect prototype. The dashboard switches from simulated to live telemetry (Web Serial, Chrome/Edge).

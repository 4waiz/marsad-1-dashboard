      Coastal asset alerts

      How detection works

      1. AS7341 samples water-leaving light at 445–680 nm + NIR during the 10-min nadir window.
      2. ESP32-S3 computes NDCI = (R708 − R665) / (R708 + R665) onboard.
      3. Pixels above threshold are tagged; a compact alert packet goes down over LoRa at the next UAE pass.
      4. Full OV5640 images are stored and downlinked selectively for confirmation.

02 · Mission

A daily bloom map
from orbit

Orbit
SSO · 550 km
i = 97.6° · 10:30 LTDN
95 min · 15 orbits/day
Platform
3U CubeSat
12-month mission
Abu Dhabi ground station
Success criterion
One usable, cloud-free image of UAE waters every day, and a bloom map to the ground team within 24 h, for 6 consecutive months.

(R708 − R665) / (R708 + R665)
